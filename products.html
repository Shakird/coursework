What We Bake

Mixed, shaped, and baked in this building every morning.

Everything below is baked in-house each morning. Prices are ranges because sizes and seasonal fillings change week to week. The chalkboard by the register always has the day’s exact list. We do not sell online; place a pre-order through the request form and pay at pickup.

Breads

Naturally leavened and long-fermented, mixed the day before they are baked. Whole loaves only. Ask at the counter and we will slice it for you.

Price range: $6.00 – $12.00

  • Signature Country Sourdough, $9.00. Rye starter, 48-hour ferment, blistered crust, open crumb.
  • Seeded Multigrain Batard, $9.50. Flax, sunflower, and sesame soaked overnight; dense and nutty.
  • Honey Rye Pan Loaf, $8.50. Soft sandwich crumb with local wildflower honey. This week’s featured item.
  • Everyday Baguette, $6.00. Crisp shell, light interior. Best within a few hours, which is rather the point of it.
  • Sharp Cheddar and Scallion Boule, $12.00. Weekends only, and usually gone by ten.

Pastries

Laminated by hand with butter, rolled and folded over two days. Sold individually or in mixed boxes of six and twelve. The boxes are what most of our commuters pre-order for a Monday meeting.

$3.25 – $6.00 each · boxes $18.00 – $34.00

  • Butter Croissant, $3.75. Twenty-seven layers, shatters when you tear it.
  • Almond Croissant, $4.75. Yesterday’s croissant, soaked and filled with frangipane. Nothing wasted.
  • Seasonal Fruit Danish, $4.50. Whatever the orchard stand has that week.
  • Cinnamon Knot, $4.00. Cardamom in the dough, coarse sugar on top.
  • Morning Bun, $4.25. Orange zest and brown sugar, baked in a muffin tin.
  • Mixed Pastry Box, $18.00 (six) or $34.00 (twelve). Baker’s choice, or tell us what to include.

Allergies and dietary notes

Our kitchen handles wheat, rye, dairy, eggs, tree nuts, and sesame every single day on shared surfaces and shared equipment. We cannot promise an allergen-free product, but we will tell you exactly what is in anything on the shelf. Just ask, or note it on the pre-order form and we will call you before we bake.
