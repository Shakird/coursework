North Star Bakery

Handmade breads, pastries, and cakes since 2011.

Welcome to the corner of Fifth and Alder

We are a small neighborhood bakery that starts the ovens at four in the morning so the shelves are full by the time you walk past. Everything we sell was mixed, shaped, and baked by hand in the back of this shop. No trucks, no warehouse, no shortcuts. Come in for a loaf, stay for the smell of it.

Planning ahead? We take pre-orders for breads, pastry boxes, and celebration cakes. Send us a request and we will hold your order at the counter.

What we’re known for

  • Long-fermented sourdough. Two days from starter to shelf, with a dark crust and an open, chewy crumb.
  • Morning pastry. Laminated croissants, seasonal fruit danish, and cinnamon knots pulled from the oven at seven.
  • Cakes for the block. Birthdays, retirements, and school bake sales, with small-batch layer cakes made to order.
  • Commuter-friendly hours. Coffee and a bag of pastry on the counter before the 7:20 train.
